## Project Summary

The bad situation: As of 2025, Spotify consisted of 31.4% of global music subscriptions, Apple Music of 12.6%, and YouTube Music of 12.4%. But sharing music across platforms is highly inconvenient, making it difficult to share music and make playlists with friends. 

What we want to build: A cross-platform playlist. When building a playlist, add either the Spotify, YouTube or Apple Music link, and the app will find the corresponding link of the other platform. Then, you can listen cross-platform. Set your preferred platform, and our app will automatically route you to that platform and play the corresponding songs.

## Issues and Concerns

- Some features we need may only be accessible to Premium users
